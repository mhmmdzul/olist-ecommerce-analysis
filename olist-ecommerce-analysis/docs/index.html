Key insights

  1. Late delivery is the main driver of bad reviews. On-time orders average 4.29 stars; late orders average 2.27, and 62% of them get 1–2 stars.
  2. Logistics breaks at demand peaks. The late rate jumped to 12% in Nov 2017 and 14–19% in Feb–Mar 2018.
  3. Almost nobody buys twice. Only 3% of customers return, so growth depends on new customers and stalled in 2018.
  4. Two high-value RFM segments hold ~56% of revenue: new big spenders (second-order offer) and lapsed big spenders (win-back).
  5. North and Northeast are underserved: ~2x longer delivery and freight at ~22% of the product price.
